# Flowline disclaimer

Flowline is an educational and exploratory traffic simulation tool. Its
driving rules are simplified and have not been calibrated or validated
against real-world data. Results are illustrative only and must not be used
for road-safety, design, legal, insurance, or engineering decisions. It is
not a substitute for professional engineering analysis. The software is
provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, including accuracy or fitness
for a particular purpose. References to standards (such as IRC) mean only
that values were taken from them for reference; the tool is not certified as
compliant with any standard.
