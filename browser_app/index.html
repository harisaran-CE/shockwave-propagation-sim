What’s under the hood?

The road is a one-dimensional chain of 7 m cells. Vehicle density is updated every 0.25 seconds using a conservative LWR traffic-flow solver and a Greenshields speed–density relationship. Playback speed changes only how quickly the animation runs.

The two lanes and car drawings help you read the traffic pattern; they are not individually simulated vehicles or a calibrated 2D model.

Inflow variability adds a seeded random demand change each second around the selected average. The seed is saved in exported runs so the sequence can be reproduced.
